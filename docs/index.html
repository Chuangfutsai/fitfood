About FitFood

FitFood is a food and cardio companion for fat-loss routines. Use the ingredient spinner to find a meal for today, log food and a daily calorie goal, then follow cardio plans consistently.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Your meal records, calorie goal, training check-ins, streaks, and preferences stay only on your device. FitFood has no account and does not collect or upload this data.

How do I back up and restore data?

On the Food page, tap the goal icon in the top-right corner. At the bottom, choose Data Backup, then Export Backup. This creates a .json file that you can save to the Files app or iCloud Drive. To restore, choose Restore from Backup, select the file, and confirm the preview. Restoring replaces your current data.

What if I get a new phone?

Export a backup on your old phone, transfer it to your new phone with AirDrop or iCloud Drive, then choose Restore from Backup in FitFood on the new phone.

Are calorie values accurate?

Recipe calories and daily calorie goals are estimates for everyday reference only; they are not medical or nutrition advice.

關於 FitFood

FitFood 是專為減脂日常打造的飲食與有氧訓練夥伴。使用食材轉盤找出今天能做的料理、記錄餐點和每日熱量目標，再搭配有氧課表穩定前進。

常見問題

我的資料存在哪裡？

餐點紀錄、每日熱量目標、訓練打卡、連續天數和偏好設定只會儲存在您的裝置上。FitFood 不建立帳號，也不會收集或上傳這些資料。

如何備份和還原？

在「吃」頁面點右上角的目標圖示，最下面選擇「資料備份」→「匯出備份」，會產生一個 .json 檔案；可存到「檔案」App 或 iCloud Drive。還原時選「從備份還原」，選好檔案、確認預覽後，就會取代目前的資料。

換手機怎麼辦？

在舊手機匯出備份檔，透過 AirDrop 或 iCloud Drive 傳到新手機，再在新手機的 FitFood 選擇「從備份還原」。

熱量資料準確嗎？

食譜熱量與每日熱量目標都是估算值，僅供日常參考，不構成醫療或營養建議。
