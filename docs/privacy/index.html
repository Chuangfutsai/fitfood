FitFood Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-12

FitFood does not collect any personal data or usage data. The app does not create accounts and does not use advertising, tracking, analytics, or data uploads.

Your meal records, calorie goal, training check-ins, streaks, and app preferences are stored only on your device. FitFood does not transmit this information to us or to third parties.

If you create, export, or keep a device backup, you are responsible for safeguarding that backup and deciding where it is stored. FitFood does not receive, manage, or have access to your backups.

If you remove the app or erase its local data, the data stored in the app may no longer be available. Because FitFood does not collect or retain your data, we cannot recover it for you.

For questions about this policy, contact: GitHub Issues

FitFood 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026-11-12

FitFood 不收集任何個人資料或使用資料。本 App 不建立帳號、不使用廣告、追蹤、分析工具或資料上傳功能。

您的餐點紀錄、每日熱量目標、訓練打卡、連續天數及 App 偏好設定，只儲存在您的裝置上。FitFood 不會將這些資料傳送給我們或任何第三方。

若您建立、匯出或保留裝置備份檔，請自行妥善保管並決定其儲存位置。FitFood 不會接收、管理或存取您的備份檔。

若您移除 App 或清除本機資料，App 內的資料可能無法再取得。由於 FitFood 不收集或保留您的資料，我們無法代為復原。

若您對本政策有任何疑問，請聯絡：GitHub Issues
