## Capture checklist

- Capture each screen once in English and once in Traditional Chinese if both localizations will receive screenshots.
- Use only the app’s actual recipe names, nutrition values, plans, equipment types, and interface.
- Do not include names, emails, photos, or other personal information in sample records.
- For screenshot 4, keep the calorie-goal value framed as an estimate; do not make a medical or guaranteed weight-loss claim.
