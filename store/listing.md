### Description — 842 characters (limit: 4,000)

FitFood is a fat-loss companion for planning meals and staying consistent with cardio training.

Open the ingredient spinner, choose what is in your fridge, and get a dish you can make today. Explore 55 home-style recipes with complete instructions, calorie and protein information, and breakfast, lunch, and dinner categories.

Keep your day in view by recording meals and setting a daily calorie goal estimated with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. Calorie values and goals are estimates and are not medical advice.

For training, choose from 8 types of cardio equipment and 24 fat-loss workout plans. Use the weekly training schedule to organize your routine, then check in to build a streak.

FitFood is available in Traditional Chinese and English.

Your data stays on your device. FitFood has no account, ads, tracking, or data collection.
