### Description — 1,238 characters (limit: 4,000)

LinkFit: Eat & Train is a fat-loss companion for planning meals, tracking your day, and staying consistent with cardio and strength training.

Open the ingredient spinner, choose what is in your fridge, and get a dish you can make today. Explore 55 home-style recipes with complete instructions, calorie and protein information, and breakfast, lunch, and dinner categories.

Start on Today: see your calorie ring, meals for the day, and today’s training check-in in one place. Record meals and set a daily calorie goal estimated with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. Calorie values and goals are estimates and are not medical advice.

Plan a week of meals in one tap, then use the automatic shopping list. Mark an item bought to add it back to your pantry inventory.

For training, choose from 8 types of cardio equipment and 24 fat-loss workout plans. Use the weekly schedule to organize your routine, then check in to build a streak. Record strength workouts as you go with weight, reps, a rest timer, personal records, and progress charts.

Export a backup file and restore it later or on a new phone.

LinkFit is available in Traditional Chinese and English. Your data stays on your device: no account, ads, tracking, or data collection.
