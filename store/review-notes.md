# App Review Notes — FitFood

FitFood does not require login, account creation, a subscription, or an internet connection to review its core features. All user-entered information remains on the device.

## How to test

1. Launch the app; proceed directly to the main experience without signing in.
2. Test meal discovery: in the “食材 Pantry” tab, check the items that are in stock; then return to the “轉盤 Wheel” tab and tap Spin to receive a meal suggestion. Open the suggested recipe to view its complete instructions, calorie value, protein value, and meal category.
3. Test meal logging: add a meal record, then view the recorded meal in the meal log.
4. Test the daily calorie goal: enter the requested inputs in the calorie-goal flow and view the estimated daily goal. The estimate uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation.
5. Test training: open the cardio equipment and workout-plan areas; choose a plan and review the weekly training schedule.
6. Test check-ins: mark a training day complete and confirm that the check-in and streak count update.
7. Test language: tap the language button in the top-right corner to switch between 繁體中文 and English.

## Important health notice

Calorie values and daily calorie goals are estimates only. They are not medical advice and are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition.

## Privacy

The app has no account, advertising, tracking, analytics, or data collection. Meal records, goals, check-ins, streaks, and preferences are stored only on the device.
