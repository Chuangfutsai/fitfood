# App Privacy Answers — FitFood

Use these answers in **App Store Connect → App Privacy** for the submitted build.

## Data Collection

**Question:** Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?

**Answer:** **No, we do not collect data from this app.**

**Reason:** FitFood does not transmit data off the device to the developer or third parties. It has no account, advertising, tracking, analytics, or data uploads. Meal records, calorie goals, training check-ins, streaks, and preferences remain on the device.

## Data Types

**Answer:** No data types selected; no follow-up data-type questions apply.

**Reason:** The app does not collect contact information, health and fitness data, financial information, location, sensitive information, contacts, user content, browsing/search history, identifiers, purchases, usage data, diagnostics, or other data from the app.

## Tracking

**Question:** Do you or your third-party partners use data from this app to track users?

**Answer:** **No.**

**Reason:** FitFood has no tracking, advertising, analytics, accounts, or third-party data sharing.

## Submission check

Before publishing, confirm the actual release build contains no SDK or service that transmits data off-device. If that changes, update these answers before submission.
